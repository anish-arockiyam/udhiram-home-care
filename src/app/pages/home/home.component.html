THE HEART BEHIND UDHIRAM

Meet Our Founder

Abarna Mohan, B.Sc. Nursing

Managing Director | Udhiram Home Care Nursing

A graduate of Saveetha College of Nursing and a University Gold Medalist, Abarna currently works as a Senior Staff Nurse in the NHS, London.

Udhiram Home Care Nursing was born from a deeply personal experience following the loss of her beloved sister to cancer. During her illness, Abarna experienced firsthand the difficulty of finding qualified, compassionate, and professional nursing care at home.

With her sister's wishes in her heart, she started Udhiram with a commitment to ensure that no family has to struggle to find quality nursing care when they need it most.

“Founded with love. Driven by purpose. Dedicated to quality care.”
